ZooTopia 2 : The Movie

The story follows Judy Hopps, a determined rabbit from the farming village of Bunnyburrow who dreams of becoming a police officer. Despite graduating at the top of her class at the Police Academy, Judy is met with skepticism by her boss, Chief Bogo, who assigns her to be a meter maid instead of a detective. While on duty, Judy meets Nick Wilde, a smooth-talking, cynical fox who makes a living running scams. Later, when given a strict 48-hour deadline by Chief Bogo to solve a missing person's case (an otter named Mr. Otterton), Judy corners Nick into helping her.

In the sequel, Judy and Nick continue their investigation, uncovering a plot to kill all the animals in the city, and eventually uncovering the true identity of their targets. As the unlikely duo investigates, they uncover a terrifying conspiracy: the missing predators have all gone "savage," reverting to their primitive, aggressive instincts. Judy and Nick must navigate the dangerous underworld of Zootopia, evade powerful criminal bosses, and solve a complex political plot to restore peace and equality to their city.
